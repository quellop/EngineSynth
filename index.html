Your location stays on this screen. The page doesn’t send it anywhere.

Use while parked or as a passenger, and switch on GPS mode before you drive. Manual mode is an on-screen simulation. GPS mode follows the car’s reported speed but can’t read the pedal, so the sound reacts after the car does. Engine sounds are synthesized approximations, not recordings.
